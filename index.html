Voice commands

Numbers can be spoken or mixed with words — “one twenty” = 120, “forty five” = 45.

  • Play / Stop — “go”, “start”, “stop”
  • Set tempo — say a number, e.g. “one hundred twenty”
  • Tempo by name — “presto”, “andante”, “allegro”… (matched even if slightly mis-heard, e.g. “moderato”)
  • Faster / Slower — “faster”, “slower”
  • Time signature — “three four”, “6/8”, “waltz”
  • Subdivision — “subdivision 2”, “triplets”
  • Tap — “tap”
  • Speed ramp — “50 to 150 by 10 every 30”, or by name “andante to presto by 10”. “stop” ends it.
  • Adjust a running ramp — re-state just one part: “every 7 seconds”, “by 8”, “up to 200”
  • Restart from a new start — “start again from 50” (keeps the same target/step)
  • Hold this tempo — “hold this tempo” (stops climbing, keeps playing)
  • Reset — “reset” (120 BPM, 4/4)
